# Twelve-Week Curriculum

**Suite:** JA21 JA Service and Protocol Language Suite 1.0.0

| Week | Theme | Reading | Deliverable |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language identity and service contracts | Chapters 1–2 | Parse headers, modules, policies, and service declarations. |
| 2 | Messages, endpoints, and errors | Chapters 3–4 | Model typed request, response, method, and error contracts. |
| 3 | Generated interfaces and compatibility | Chapters 5–6 | Compare server, client, test-double, serialization, and version views. |
| 4 | Commands, events, and protocols | Chapters 7–8 | Build and validate a conversational state machine. |
| 5 | Streaming and reliability | Chapters 9–10 | Test bidirectional flow, backpressure, retry, backoff, deadlines, and idempotency. |
| 6 | Resource governance | Chapter 11 | Design quota and rate-limit accounting with deterministic boundary tests. |
| 7 | Identity, authority, and capabilities | Chapters 12–13 | Separate authentication, authorization, and network capability evidence. |
| 8 | Transport and discovery | Chapters 14–15 | Implement local and offline transport plans and compatibility-aware discovery. |
| 9 | Validators and compiler frontend | Chapters 16–18 | Produce typed ASTs, semantic judgments, and stable diagnostics. |
| 10 | Runtime and replay | Chapter 19 | Create deterministic dispatch traces and MCRT receipts. |
| 11 | Optimization and certification | Chapters 20–21 | Prove safe transforms and assemble a certification packet. |
| 12 | Capstone | Chapter 22 | Present an air-gap-capable service/protocol toolchain. |

## Assessment

- Weekly laboratories: 35%
- Midterm protocol and reliability review: 20%
- Certification evidence packet: 15%
- Capstone toolchain: 30%
