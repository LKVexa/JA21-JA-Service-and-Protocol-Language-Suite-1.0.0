# Instructor Guide

Teach the suite as contract engineering, not framework syntax. Require students to separate expected corpus evidence from native results. Grade source correctness, semantic reasoning, policy safety, deterministic testing, evidence continuity, and publication clarity.

## Recommended grading rubric

- Contract and type correctness: 20%
- Protocol and reliability reasoning: 20%
- Authority and fail-closed behavior: 20%
- Compiler/runtime evidence: 20%
- Reproducibility and presentation: 20%
